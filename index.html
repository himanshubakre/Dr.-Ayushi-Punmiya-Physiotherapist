Physiotherapy Care Focused on Your Recovery

Dr. Ayushi Punmiya holds an MPTh in Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy and works with patients to build treatment plans suited to their condition, goals and daily routine — from managing pain to returning to sport.

  • MPTh — Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

    Postgraduate specialisation in assessing and treating conditions of the muscles, joints and spine.

  • FIFA Diploma in Football Medicine

    Additional training in sports medicine, informing the approach to sports injury rehabilitation.

  • Sports Injury Rehabilitation

    Structured rehabilitation support for athletes and active individuals working toward return-to-activity.

  • Advanced Rehabilitation Techniques

    Kinesiology taping, dry needling, Mulligan techniques and clinical neurodynamics, applied where appropriate to the individual.
